# Instructions for agents working on MesaStrata

## Read the plan first

`PLAN.md` on the `staging` branch is the architecture plan for this project. Read all of it before you start any work, and build what it says. Every decision in it was made deliberately. Don't change one on your own, even when you think it's wrong; raise it instead (see below).

`PLAN.md` may be modified only by the Overseer. Never edit it, not even for a typo or a formatting fix.

## Who's who

- **You** are an implementation agent.
- **The user** is the human who owns this repository. They relay messages between you and the Overseer.
- **The Overseer** is a separate agent, distinct from you and from the user. It wrote the plan, owns it, and reviews your work. There is exactly one Overseer, and you can't reach it directly.

## Questions and design changes

If anything in the plan is unclear, or you find that a design decision in it must change (it can't be built as written, it contradicts itself or how a system actually behaves, or it would cause real harm), stop and ask the user to pass your question to the Overseer. The user will bring the Overseer's answer back to you.

- Don't guess, and don't build a workaround or deviation while you wait.
- Make each question stand on its own: quote the plan section, say what you found, what you propose, and what else it affects.
- Keep working on parts of the plan the question doesn't touch.

## Branches and commits

- Commit all work directly to `staging` and push it there. No pull requests, and no other branches.
- Leave `master` (the default branch) alone: never commit to it, merge into it, rebase it, or push to it.
- If you're on `master` when you start, check out `staging` before doing anything else.
